About

I'm a product manager with ~6 years across fintech, consumer, and healthcare — most recently defining billing, KYB, and payout infrastructure for a treasury platform used by partner-merchants across APAC. I care about finding the smallest workflow change that removes the most friction, whether that's a conditional verification flow, a commission breakdown, or a launch checklist a team actually follows. I started as a mechanical engineer, which is probably why I still think in systems and tolerances more than features.
